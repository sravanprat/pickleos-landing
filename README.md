PickleOS

Camera-free sensor training for the pickleball kitchen line.

PickleOS gives players real-time coaching while they play: a haptic buzz through the paddle the instant a foot fault or open paddle face happens, audio tips between points, and a full breakdown after the match. No cameras, no $250 smart paddles, no GPS guesswork. Sensors clip onto the gear you already own and range against two net-post anchors using UWB.
