Setup, start to first correction

Three steps. Under two minutes. No app tutorial required.

01

Anchor the net

Snap the two UWB transceivers onto either net post. They triangulate against each other to establish your court's exact boundary geometry.

02

Clip in

Attach the footwear sensors and the paddle handle module. Nothing to charge mid-session, nothing that changes your grip.

03

Play

The system starts timestamping every shoe position and paddle contact against the net anchors the moment you start rallying.

What the sensors actually give you

Correction while you're playing. A full breakdown once you're not.

Instant · through the paddle
0–50ms

A foot fault or an off-balance strike triggers a specific vibration pattern in the handle before your next split-step, correcting the habit while your body still remembers the motion.

Delayed · through your earbuds
Dead-ball only

Strategic notes on backswing, weight shift, or paddle angle wait for three full seconds of no motion before speaking, so nothing ever talks over an active point.

After · on your phone
Full match

Every foot position and paddle contact stays timestamped against the net for the whole session, so you can see exactly which rallies your feet and hands stopped agreeing with each other.
